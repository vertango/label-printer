First-time setup
  1. Turn the printer on. On the tablet open Settings → Connections → Bluetooth, tap JD-168BT_xxxx and pair it (PIN 0000 if asked).
  2. Close the Jadens app so it isn't holding the connection.
  3. On this page tap Connect printer and choose the JD-168BT. Chrome remembers it, so next time it reconnects by itself when you print.
  4. To get the app on your home screen and in the Share menu: tap Chrome's ⋮ menu → Add to home screen → Install. Then, from a downloaded label, tap Share → Label Printer.
  5. Tap Test print below. The border should sit just inside the label edges. If it's off, use Move print in Settings.

Printing the Outlet Analytics packing list
  1. In Outlet Analytics open Packing list and tap Print 4x6.
  2. In the print screen choose Save as PDF as the printer and save it.
  3. Here, tap Add packing list and pick that PDF. Long lists print over several labels, in order.

The sample is made-up stock, laid out like the Outlet Analytics packing list, so you can test without real orders.
